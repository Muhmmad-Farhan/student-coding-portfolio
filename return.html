Government College of Technology, Sangla Hill


Welcome to GCT sangla Hil

Government College of Technology (GCT) Sangla Hill is a well-known technical college in Punjab, Pakistan. It offers quality education in various engineering and technology fields. The college has experienced teachers, modern labs, and a friendly environment for students to learn and grow.


Importance of Technical Education

Technical education teaches practical skills and knowledge needed for jobs in engineering, computers, electronics, and other industries. It helps students become skilled workers, solve real-world problems, and contribute to the development of society. Technical education also improves career opportunities and supports innovation and technology growth.
